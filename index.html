American Red Cross

The American Red Cross offers disaster relief and recovery services, including emergency shelters, food, and emotional support. They also provide resources for preparing, responding, and recovering from various emergencies.

FEMA

FEMA provides financial assistance and direct services to eligible individuals and households affected by disasters. They offer programs like the Individuals and Households Program (IHP) to help with uninsured or underinsured necessary expenses and serious needs.

National Voluntary Organizations Active in Disaster

NVOAD coordinates planning efforts by many voluntary organizations responding to disaster. They provide a platform for organizations to share knowledge and resources throughout the disaster cycle.
