## Product Signal

ResQnet reflects curiosity around mission-driven software: tools that help people coordinate, prioritize, and act under pressure. The project frames emergency response as a systems problem where interface clarity, task flow, and decision support matter as much as the underlying implementation.

## What I Explored

- Coordination workflows for high-pressure environments
- Product thinking around response, prioritization, and handoffs
- How software can make complex operations easier to reason about
- Fast prototyping around real-world systems problems
