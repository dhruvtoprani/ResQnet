ResQnet is a systems prototype exploring coordination, response workflows, and decision support for real-world operational environments.

It frames response coordination as a product problem: how do users prioritize, hand off work, and understand what needs attention when the environment is complex?

**Links:** [Source repository](https://github.com/dhruvtoprani/ResQnet)

## Product Signal

ResQnet reflects curiosity around mission-driven software: tools that help people coordinate, prioritize, and act under pressure. The project emphasizes interface clarity, task flow, and decision support as core parts of a usable system.

## What I Explored

- Coordination workflows for high-pressure environments
- Product thinking around response, prioritization, and handoffs
- How software can make complex operations easier to reason about
- Fast prototyping around real-world systems problems
